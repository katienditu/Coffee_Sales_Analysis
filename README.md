### Project Overview

The coffee sales analysis project focused on extracting insights from a multi-year dataset containing detailed information on customer orders, product types, roast preferences, packaging sizes, and country-wise sales performance. Using Microsoft Excel, the data was cleaned through duplicate removal, consistent formatting, and conversion into structured tables. Advanced Excel functions like XLOOKUP, INDEX MATCH, and multiple IF statements were applied for accurate data retrieval and logic handling. A dynamic dashboard was then developed to visualize key metrics such as total sales over time, top-performing countries (United States, Ireland, and United Kingdom), and the top five customers by sales volume. Interactive elements like slicers, timelines, and pivot tables enabled users to filter results by roast type, loyalty card usage, and coffee size, ultimately supporting data-driven decisions for business growth.

### Dataset Description

The dataset, contained in a multi-sheet Excel file, includes the following:

- Orders: Sales transactions with date, product, and customer details.
- Customers: Customer names and associated IDs.
- Products: Product names, sizes, and roast types.
- Sales Metrics: Revenue by order, country, and customer.
- The data spans from 2019 to 2022 and covers sales in the United States, Ireland, and the United Kingdom.

### Data Cleaning

To ensure high-quality analysis, the following cleaning steps were applied:

- Removed duplicate records from the orders and customer sheets.
- Checked for missing values and ensured consistency across date and text fields.
- Converted raw data ranges into Excel Tables for better referencing and analysis.
- Applied Number Formatting for currency and date values for easier interpretation.
- Standardized column naming for clarity and uniformity across formulas.

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The goal of the EDA phase was to understand data distributions, trends, and anomalies:

1. Created Pivot Tables to summarize sales by country, roast type, customer, and time.
2. Identified peak months and years with the highest sales activity.
3. Compared performance across loyalty card holders vs non-holders.
4. Explored which roast types and package sizes performed best over time.
5. Used slicers and timelines to detect seasonal patterns in customer purchases.

### Data Analysis

A variety of Excel formulas and logic operations were applied:

1. Used XLOOKUP and INDEX MATCH for accurate data merging across sheets.
2. Applied Multiple IF statements to categorize customers and conditions.
3. Calculated total sales using the multiplication of quantity and price per product.
4. Segmented sales by customer, product type, and region for granular analysis.
5. Developed a responsive structure that automatically updated with new data inputs.

### Dashboard Design

The dashboard was built with user interactivity and clarity in mind:

- Used Pivot Charts for visualizing sales trends over time and by geography.
- Inserted Timelines to allow users to filter by order year and month.
- Added Slicers for roast type, loyalty card status, and package size.
- Visualized Top 5 Customers using bar charts for quick business insight.
- Ensured all visuals were professionally formatted with consistent themes and spacing.

### Key Insights

From the analysis, several insights emerged:

1. The United States consistently led in sales volume and revenue.
2. Dark roast coffee was the most popular across all countries.
3. Loyalty cardholders contributed to a higher average sales per transaction.
4. Sales peaked during the middle of each year, suggesting seasonal preferences.
5. A few customers accounted for a significant portion of total revenue.

### Recommendations

Based on the insights, the following actions were suggested:

1. Focus marketing efforts in the U.S., especially around mid-year periods.
2. Promote loyalty programs to boost customer retention and sales.
3. Expand dark roast offerings and introduce combo packs with top-selling sizes.
4. Target top customers with personalized discounts or early product releases.
5. Use interactive dashboards for regular performance monitoring.

### Limitations

Some constraints were noted during the project:

- Lack of demographic data (age, location) limited customer profiling.
- Data only included historical sales; no forecast or inventory data was present.
- There may be slight inconsistencies if manual entries were involved in the original files.
- Dashboard scalability is limited in Excel for very large datasets.
